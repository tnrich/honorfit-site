HonorFit Blocker Support Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

HonorFit Blocker ("the app") is a self-control tool that blocks apps you choose until you earn screen time by logging exercise.

The short version: the app collects no data. Nothing you do in the app ever leaves your device.

What the app stores (on your device only)

  • The list of apps you choose to block
  • Your logged reps, streaks, and earned screen-time balance
  • Your settings (for example, how many minutes each rep is worth)

This information is stored in the app's private storage on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app. It is never transmitted anywhere.

What the app does NOT do

  • No accounts, sign-ins, or profiles
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs
  • No data collection, transmission, sale, or sharing of any kind

Usage Access and the lock overlay (Android)

On Android the app uses the Usage Access permission (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS) to detect when an app you have blocked comes to the foreground, and the Display over other apps permission (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW) to draw the lock screen over it. HonorFit does not use an Accessibility Service, so it has no ability to read your screen content, keystrokes, or text. The only signal it receives is the package name of the foreground app, which is compared against your blocklist on-device and never stored beyond that check or transmitted. The QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES permission populates the in-app blocklist picker with your installed apps; that list is never transmitted.

Installed app list

To let you choose which apps to block, the app lists the apps installed on your device. This list is shown only inside the app's picker and never leaves your device.

Notifications

The app may schedule local notifications (for example, when your earned screen time runs out). These are generated entirely on-device.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

This website

This website (not the app) uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. It sets no cookies, does not fingerprint or track individual visitors, and collects no personal data. It only records aggregate information such as which pages are visited, the referring site, and the country.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be shipped with the app and posted at the same URL as this document.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on GitHub or see the support page.
