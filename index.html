How it works

  1. Pick the apps to block.
  2. Do pushups, situps, pull ups, or squats and log them on the rep wheels.
  3. Every rep deposits screen time into your balance.
  4. Your balance only counts down while you're actually inside a blocked app — switch away and the clock stops.
  5. When the balance hits zero, the apps lock again and HonorFit drops you back on the rep wheels to earn more.

The honor system

No proof required

No camera. No motion tracking. You log your own reps — if you cheat, the only person you're lying to is the one doing zero pushups.

Zero AI

None. Not a drop. No AI rep counting, no machine-learning "form detection", no cloud processing, no chatbot coach. Just you, the floor, and your word.

Private by design

Everything stays on your device. No accounts, no analytics, no data collection. The app can't read your screen — it only sees which app is in the foreground.

Common questions

How does HonorFit know I actually did the pushups?

It doesn’t. HonorFit is an honor system — no camera, no motion tracking, no AI rep counting. You log your own reps. If you cheat, the only person you’re lying to is the one doing zero pushups.

Does HonorFit work on both iPhone and Android?

Yes. On iPhone it uses Apple’s Screen Time (Family Controls) to shield the apps you pick. On Android it runs a foreground service that watches which app is on screen and covers blocked apps with a lock overlay.

Does HonorFit collect my data?

No. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no network calls. Everything is stored on your device. HonorFit cannot read your screen content — it only sees which app is in the foreground.

What does HonorFit cost?

Blocking, workouts, and stats are free, with earned time banked up to 20 minutes. HonorFit Pro removes the cap and adds custom exercises, timed workouts, saved routines, hard mode, a daily baseline, and pause mode, for $3.00 per month or $19.99 once.
