The honor system

No Clunky Camera Tracking

No camera to prop up, no motion tracking, no AI rep counting. Just you, the floor, and your word.

Private by design

Everything stays on your device. No accounts, no analytics, no data collection. HonorFit can't read your screen — it only knows which app you're in.

Features

Four exercises

Pushups, situps, pull ups, and squats — each with its own independent screen-time rate.

Live balance & streaks

Watch your earned time count down in real time, and keep your daily streak alive.

Stats

Total reps, workouts, best streak, a per-exercise breakdown, and a 14-day chart.

Pro

No cap on banked time (free stops at 20 minutes), plus custom exercises and rep values, timed workouts, a daily baseline, pause mode, and hard mode.

Common questions

How does HonorFit know I actually did the pushups?

It doesn’t. If you cheat, the only person you’re lying to is the one doing zero pushups.

Does HonorFit work on both iPhone and Android?

Yes. On iPhone, Apple’s Screen Time does the blocking; on Android, HonorFit does.

Does HonorFit collect my data?

No. No accounts, no analytics — everything stays on your phone.

What does HonorFit cost?

Free. Pro lifts the 20-minute cap on banked time and adds the extras — $3.00 a month, or $19.99 once.
