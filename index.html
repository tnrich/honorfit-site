Why I built this

Hey y’all! I needed a way to cap my own phone use, so I downloaded one of those “do pushups to unlock your phone” apps. Loved the idea! What I didn’t love was propping my phone up so it could watch me do the pushups. Super annoying, and kinda embarrassing when people see you doing pushups at a gas station lol.

So I built HonorFit. Same idea, just on the honor system. You do your reps, log them, and that buys you screen time. No camera watching you.

It’s definitely not for everyone. If you know you’d cheat, the camera ones are probably better for you! But I’ve been using it for a few months and it’s honestly changed how much I scroll.

— Thomas Willheim, biotech software developer on California’s Central Coast

The honor system

No Clunky Camera Tracking

No camera to prop up, no motion tracking, no AI rep counting. Just you, the floor, and your word.

Private by design

Everything stays on your device. No accounts, no analytics, no data collection. HonorFit can't read your screen — it only knows which app you're in.

Common questions

How does HonorFit know I actually did the pushups?

It doesn’t. If you cheat, the only person you’re lying to is the one doing zero pushups.

Does HonorFit work on both iPhone and Android?

Yes. On iPhone, Apple’s Screen Time does the blocking; on Android, HonorFit does.

Why is it called HonorFit?

Honor + fit: it runs on the honor system, and it gets you fit. Some people write it as “Honor Fit” — same app. HonorFit is an indie app and isn’t affiliated with HONOR phones, watches or the HONOR Health app.

Does HonorFit collect my data?

No. No accounts, no analytics — everything stays on your phone.

What languages is HonorFit available in?

English, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, German, French and Japanese. It uses your phone’s language automatically, and you can switch any time in Settings › Language.

What does HonorFit cost?

Free. Pro lifts the 20-minute cap on banked time and adds the extras — $3.00 a month, or $19.99 once.
