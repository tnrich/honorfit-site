HonorFit Blocker

Your scroll isn't free anymore. 💪

HonorFit locks the apps that steal your time — Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, whatever you choose — until you've earned them with exercise.

Zero AI — not a drop No camera — honor system No data collection — everything on-device

How it works

  1. Pick the apps to block.
  2. Do pushups, situps, pull ups, or squats and log them on the rep wheels.
  3. Every rep deposits screen time into your balance.
  4. Your balance only counts down while you're actually inside a blocked app — switch away and the clock stops.
  5. When the balance hits zero, the apps lock again and HonorFit drops you back on the rep wheels to earn more.

See it in action

The honor system

No proof required

No camera. No motion tracking. You log your own reps — if you cheat, the only person you're lying to is the one doing zero pushups.

Zero AI

None. Not a drop. No AI rep counting, no machine-learning "form detection", no cloud processing, no chatbot coach. Just you, the floor, and your word.

Private by design

Everything stays on your device. No accounts, no analytics, no data collection. The app can't read your screen — it only sees which app is in the foreground.

Features

Four exercises

Pushups, situps, pull ups, and squats — each with its own independent screen-time rate.

Live balance & streaks

Watch your earned time count down in real time, and keep your daily streak alive.

Workout history

Total reps and full workout history, so you can see the work stack up.

Pro

Customize how many minutes each rep of each exercise is worth.
