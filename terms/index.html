The service

HonorFit Blocker is a self-control tool. It blocks apps you select until you log exercise reps, which earn screen time. Rep logging is on the honor system: the app does not verify exercise and makes no health, fitness, or medical claims. Exercise at your own risk and consult a professional if you have any health concerns.
